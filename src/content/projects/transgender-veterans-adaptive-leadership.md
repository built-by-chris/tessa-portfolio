## About this project

This project treats equitable access to VA care as an adaptive organizational challenge rather than a problem that can be solved through technical compliance alone. It examines referral pathways, staff responsibilities, institutional knowledge, communication, veteran trust, and accountability across clinical and administrative systems.

The proposed intervention combines facility-level workflow assessment, confidential stakeholder input, limited pilot implementation, recurring review, and outcome measures such as referral completion, navigation consistency, continuity, and corrective action.

> **Portfolio status:** Draft presentation. The project is staged privately while its public framing and supporting materials are reviewed.
