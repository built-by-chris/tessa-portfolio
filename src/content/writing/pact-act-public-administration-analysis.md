## About this work

This analysis examines the PACT Act as both a policy change and an administrative implementation challenge. It applies traditional managerial, New Public Management, political, and legal approaches to evaluate the demands created by expanded eligibility, presumptive service connection, claims processing, health-care access, oversight, and organizational capacity.

A central tension in the paper is that reducing administrative burden for veterans can simultaneously increase administrative burden on the VA. The analysis therefore treats implementation capacity, fairness, timeliness, legal entitlement, and accountability as interconnected public-administration problems.
